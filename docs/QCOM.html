QUALCOMM Incorporated engages in the development and commercialization of foundational technologies for the wireless industry in China, the United States, South Korea, and internationally. It operates through three segments: Qualcomm CDMA Technologies (QCT); Qualcomm Technology Licensing (QTL); and Qualcomm Strategic Initiatives (QSI). The QCT segment develops and supplies integrated circuits and system software with connectivity and computing technologies for use in mobile devices; automotive systems for connectivity, digital cockpit, and ADAS/AD; and IoT, including personal AI and compute devices and industrial, networking, and robotics products.
