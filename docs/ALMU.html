Aeluma, Inc. develops photonic and electronic technologies for communications and sensing in the United States. The company is involved in development of photodetectors, photodetector arrays, lasers, optical amplifiers, and other technologies. Its products are used in telecom, artificial intelligence, datacom, mobile, defense and aerospace, robotics, automotive, augmented reality/virtual reality, and quantum applications.
