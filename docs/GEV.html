The company operates through three segments: Power, Wind, and Electrification. The Power segment designs, manufactures, and services gas, nuclear, hydro, and steam technologies. It serves industrial, government, and other customers. The Wind segment offers wind generation technologies, including onshore and offshore wind turbines and blades.
