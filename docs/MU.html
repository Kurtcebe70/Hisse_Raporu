Micron Technology, Inc. designs, develops, manufactures, and sells memory and storage products in the United States, Taiwan, Japan, Mainland China, Hong Kong, Europe, and internationally. The company operates through the Cloud Memory Business Unit (CMBU); Core Data Center Business Unit (CDCB); Mobile and Client Business Unit (MCBU); and Automotive and Embedded Business Unit (AEBU) segments. It provides dynamic random access memory (DRAM) semiconductor devices comprising high-bandwidth memory, double data rate, low-power DRAM, and graphics DRAM.
