LightPath Technologies, Inc. designs, develops, manufactures, and distributes optical systems and assemblies in the United States, the United Kingdom, Israel, China, Rest of Europe, Rest of Asia and internationally. It offers optical components, assemblies and sub-systems, including precision molded glass aspheric optics, thermal imaging assemblies, molded and diamond-turned infrared aspheric lenses, spherical lenses, and other optical components used in products that manipulate light; and infrared products, including catalog and custom infrared optics. The company also offers cooled and uncooled camera systems and modules for long range surveillance and detection, and engineering services. …
